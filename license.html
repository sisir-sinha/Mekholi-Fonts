3. Use in websites, books & commercial work

ActivityPractical rule
Personal or commercial documents, graphics and print workAllowed. Documents made with the font do not have to use the OFL.
Self-hosting or embedding web fontsAllowed. Preserve the OFL and notices with distributed font assets.
Embedding in PDFs, e-books, apps or other softwareAllowed under the OFL’s terms; include required notices/license when distributing the Font Software.
Bundling fonts with commercial softwareAllowed while preserving the font’s OFL license and required notices.
Selling the font file by itselfNot allowed under OFL condition 1.
Changing the font and redistributing the resultAllowed under the OFL; retain notices, keep the OFL, and respect Reserved Font Names.
Claiming that upstream authors endorse your modified fontNot allowed without permission; factual attribution is permitted.

You do not have to place a font credit in every image or document merely because you used the font. Distinguish using a font to create a document from redistributing the font files themselves.

4. Reserved Font Names

Mekholi is a Reserved Font Name for this project. Kalpurush remains an upstream Reserved Font Name.

When distributing a modified version, do not use a reserved name as its primary user-visible font name unless you have explicit written permission from the corresponding copyright holder(s). Change the internal family, full and PostScript names consistently; retain the copyright/RFN notices in attribution.

Using unmodified Mekholi in your website, artwork or document does not require renaming it. An RFN is an OFL naming condition; it is not proof of a registered trademark, uniqueness of the design, or SIL certification. For edge cases such as format conversion and technical subsetting, consult the official RFN guidance.

5. Redistribute or make a derivative

  1. Keep the relevant copyright statements, Reserved Font Name declarations and complete OFL 1.1 text with the Font Software.
  2. If you modify and redistribute it, use a new primary family name unless the corresponding rights holders give permission to retain reserved names.
  3. Describe your actual contributions and retain accurate upstream attribution. Add a contribution-specific copyright notice if appropriate.
  4. Keep the modified Font Software under the OFL. Do not impose a conflicting proprietary license or sell a font component by itself.
  5. Update the internal copyright, licensing and naming metadata, package notices and FONTLOG as applicable.

The OFL does not require you to publish private modifications or distribute source simply because you make a change. If you do redistribute Font Software, its license conditions apply. Read the full license and FAQ for your distribution scenario.
